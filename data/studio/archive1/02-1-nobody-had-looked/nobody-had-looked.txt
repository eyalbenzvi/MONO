Title: Nobody Had Looked
Category: Engravings
Family: flea
Subject: Robert Hooke's 1665 engraving of a flea from Micrographia, shown close: the head with its eye, the plated thorax and the two front legs with their claws, Hooke's plate letters kept, with the caption "Nobody had looked this closely before." under it
Style: Engraving line traced from a library scan, with a bold serif caption
Medium: Archive engraving, traced and set by MONO
Description: In 1665 Robert Hooke put a flea under his microscope and engraved what he saw, armour plates, bristles, claws and all. London had never seen anything like it.
Tee colours: White
Print size: 21.2 × 33.6 cm
Keywords: flea, Hooke, Micrographia, microscope, 1665, engraving, insect, science, close-up, line art
Model: Archive
