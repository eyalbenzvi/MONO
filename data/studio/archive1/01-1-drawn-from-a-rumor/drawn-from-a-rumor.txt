Title: Drawn from a Rumor
Category: Brush & Woodblock
Family: rhinoceros
Subject: Albrecht Dürer's 1515 woodcut rhinoceros, the armoured beast he cut without ever seeing one, from a letter and a sketch sent from Lisbon: its head, horn, scaled legs and plated shoulder, with his own "1515 RHINOCERVS" and monogram, and the caption "DRAWN FROM A RUMOR." under it
Style: Woodcut line traced from a museum scan, with a classical-capital caption
Medium: Public-domain woodcut, traced and set by MONO
Description: Dürer never saw a rhinoceros. He cut this one in 1515 from a letter and a rough sketch, armour plates and all, and for more than two centuries it was what Europe believed a rhinoceros looked like.
Tee colours: White
Print size: 25.5 × 34.1 cm
Keywords: rhinoceros, rhino, Dürer, Durer, woodcut, 1515, Renaissance, rumor, armour, animal, line art
Model: Archive
Credit and source: Albrecht Dürer, The Rhinoceros, 1515, woodcut. Cleveland Museum of Art, 1993.9, open access (CC0): https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1993.9. Traced to one ink, cropped and captioned by MONO.
