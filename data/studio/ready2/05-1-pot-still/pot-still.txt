Subject: A copper pot still drawn in cutaway over a wood fire in a brick hearth, its parts named: the fermented wash boiling in the pot, the cut-away neck showing the vapour's path, the swan neck, the lyne arm (vapour pipe), the manway, the worm tub (condenser) with its copper worm coiled in cooling water, cold water in and warm water out, the condensate (spirit) dripping into a jug for the distillate
