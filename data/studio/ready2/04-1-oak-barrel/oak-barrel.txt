Title: Oak Barrel
Category: Engravings
Family: cooperage
Subject: A cooper's drawing of an oak barrel: its elevation with dimensions (900 mm tall, heads 570 mm across), the stave construction in an exploded view with a stave's sizes (24 staves), the top, middle and bottom hoops, the riveted lap joint and its rivet, and a material key: European oak (Quercus robur / Quercus petraea) staves and heads, galvanised steel hoops, steel rivets
Style: Technical drawing with hatching and dimension lines
Medium: An illustration made with a generative tool, retouched and converted to one ink

Tee colours: Black (printed in white ink) and White (printed in black ink); leads with Black
