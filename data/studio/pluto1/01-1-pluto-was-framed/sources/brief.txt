Idea: "PLUTO WAS FRAMED" — seed-based 50-idea competition, round 3 (100 farthest-point seeds, 30% marked "made for white ink on a BLACK tee"; ranked on the printed text only by five uncalibrated persona rankers), ranked #2 (7.8); chosen by the owner. The idea named a Voyager probe; Voyager never went to Pluto, so the probe is New Horizons (flyby 14 July 2015).
Sources: NASA PIA19857 (New Horizons, Pluto in natural colour, public domain): PIA19857.png. PIA19952 (enhanced colour) was tried first and dropped: its heart is no brighter than the north. Google Gemini: gemini-probe.jpg (New Horizons line drawing, round 5, at the owner's suggestion after four code-drawn probes failed to read).
Build: python build.py params.json <out> — Pluto: 0.5 B + 0.35 G + 0.15 R, levels 0.2–0.76 γ1.55, dodge (the smooth bright plain in the south +0.35) and burn (north −25%), floor 0.12 so the whole disc reads; 1.4 mm clustered-dot screen at 45°, dots ≥ 0.52 mm, tone ≤ 72%; torn print with a 6 mm light-grey border, taped. Letters: 7 faces (Libre Caslon, Oswald, Playfair, Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono, Cinzel, UnifrakturMaguntia), each on a torn scrap: outline, 40% screen with the letter knocked out, or 66% "paper" screen; sizes, tilts and baselines varied. Probe: Gemini drawing skeletonised and redrawn at 0.7 mm, cut out with a 2.5 mm halo and keyline. White ink, delivered as -black.png (black tee only).
