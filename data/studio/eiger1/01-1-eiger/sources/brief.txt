This is a design for a $50 t-shirt printed in one ink, as one print on the BACK of the shirt: the Eiger (Bernese Alps, 3967 m) at night, as a white-ink engraving on a black tee, drawn from the real terrain (swisstopo's swissALTI3D elevation model, 2 m grid) as seen from the west-north-west, near Kleine Scheidegg, with the relief raised by 15% as the Alpine panorama painters did: the summit pyramid, the north face on the left in deep shadow, the moonlit west flank on the right running up to the summit, the skyline as a fine line, stars above. Everything is engraved horizontal lines: thick where the moon lights the rock; in shadow they keep the press minimum and break into dashes; the drawing dies away at the bottom and sides as an oval engraver's vignette whose lines grow further apart and end one by one. Five pairs of white dots in black halos on the north face are the headlamps of rope teams climbing it. No words. It is sold on a black tee in white ink only.

Idea competition div12 (open subject, 200 ideas from ten writers, six rankers), idea #22, average 7.83 (first). Made end to end without the owner, entirely in code (no generative image): fetch.py downloads the terrain, vox.py renders it column by column (params-view.json), style.py engraves it (params-style.json), place.py sets it in the print area. Five panel rounds: 4.58, 7.42, 7.17, 7.17, 7.50; this version entered the catalogue at 7.50.
