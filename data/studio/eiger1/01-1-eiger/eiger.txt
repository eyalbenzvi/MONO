Title: Eiger
Category: Engravings
Family: mountains
Subject: The Eiger (3,967 m, Bernese Alps) at night, seen from the west-north-west near Kleine Scheidegg: the summit pyramid, the north face on the left in deep shadow, the moonlit west flank on the right running up to the summit, the skyline as a fine line and stars above. On the north face, the headlamps of five rope teams climb towards the summit; the drawing fades out at its foot and sides in an oval vignette
Style: Engraving in horizontal lines, white on black
Medium: Drawn in code from the real terrain (swissALTI3D elevation model), engraved to one ink by MONO
Description: The Nordwand at night, with five rope teams' headlamps on it. The mountain is the real one: every line comes from swisstopo's two-metre survey of the Eiger, cut like an old engraving and lit only by the moon.
Tee colours: Black (printed in white ink)
Print size: 26.5 × 25.5 cm

Model: code
