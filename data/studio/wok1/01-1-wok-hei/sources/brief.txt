This is a design for a $50 t-shirt printed in one ink, as one print on the BACK of the shirt: a realistic photograph, in the shop's halftone dots, of a carbon-steel wok mid-toss over a roaring burner at night: a sheet of flame rolls over the lip, fried rice, whole prawns and spring-onion rings hang frozen in the air above it, and the cook's hand grips the handle at the lower right (only the hand: the arm is left in the dark). Only what the flame lights is printed; everything else is the black shirt. The hand fades out softly towards the right edge, and the burner fades out at the bottom. No words. About 23 × 33.6 cm on the back. It is sold on a black tee in white ink only.

Idea competition div11 (open subject), idea #45, average 8.17 of six rankers (tied first). The owner asked for a realistic photograph, not an illustration, and made gemini-wok.png with Gemini; the cook's second forearm was erased at the owner's request. Five panel rounds: 6.42, 7.33, 7.17, 7.92, 7.92; this version (round 5: hand faded past the knuckles, outer bowl lifted) entered the catalogue at 7.92.
