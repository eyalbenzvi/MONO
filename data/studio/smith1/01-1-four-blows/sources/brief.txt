This is a design for a $50 t-shirt printed in one ink, as one print on the BACK of the shirt: a 35 mm film contact strip, running down the spine (about 13.6 cm wide × 35.4 cm tall, starting just under the collar), with four frames of the same blacksmith at night in his smithy, lit only by the forge: 1) pulling a glowing bar from the fire with tongs, 2) hammer raised high over the anvil, 3) the blow landing in a fan of sparks, 4) the bar plunged into a water barrel in a cloud of steam. The photograph is in the shop's halftone dots; only what the fire lights is printed, the rest is the black shirt. Sprocket holes run down both edges of the strip and small frame numbers 1 2 3 4 sit under the frames. No other words. It is sold on a black tee in white ink only.

Idea competition div10 (manual labour, after the owner's reference photograph of a 1930s docker), idea #22, average 7.50 of six rankers. Frames 1-3 from gemini-smith-2.png (a 2x2 grid), frame 4 from gemini-smith-quench.png; the strip, gaps and numbers are drawn in build.py. Six panel rounds, best average 7.25 (round 6); this version (frame 4 retouched: staves lifted, steam drawn from its threads only) entered the catalogue by the owner's decision below the 7.5 bar.
