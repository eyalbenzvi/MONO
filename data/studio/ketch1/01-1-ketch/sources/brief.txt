This is a design for a $50 t-shirt printed in one ink, as one print on the BACK of the shirt: a fine pen-and-ink drawing of a two-masted ketch under full sail on still water, seen from the side, bowsprit to the left. Mast, booms, spreaders and hull are firm black lines; the sails are light outlines with their seams drawn as rows of dots; the hull has thin plank lines; the water below is broken horizontal lines and a stippled reflection that fades out downward. Above it, centred, the shop's heading in letter-spaced capitals: KETCH, under it smaller: Maritime · Sail plan, and a thin rule. About 21.5 × 32.6 cm (the drawing) under the heading. It is sold on a white tee in black ink.

The owner's two pictures of a ketch on a shirt (ref-engraving: the heading idea; ref-soft: the drawing) were redrawn by Google Gemini as gemini-ketch.png. One panel round on an earlier build of this drawing (average 6.25); the owner then chose this drawing unchanged with the heading, dots and water from the Spray rounds, and put it in the catalogue without a further panel.
