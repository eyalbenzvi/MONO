Title: Ketch
Category: Engravings

Subject: A fine pen-and-ink drawing of a two-masted ketch under full sail on still water, seen from the side, bowsprit to the left: firm spars and hull with thin plank lines, the sails light outlines with their seams in rows of round dots, a waterline, broken water lines and a stippled reflection that fades out downward. Description: A ketch carries her sail on two masts, the taller forward, so the canvas is split into pieces a small crew can handle. Drawn here the way a sail plan is: every seam, stay and plank.
